BidPilot

Pre-Release • In Development
Coming soon to the App Store

BidPilot is an Orion GLE product for tradespeople and contractors, built around customer records, quotations, estimating, accepted-job scheduling, and practical export tools.

The app is still in active build, with public release details being staged ahead of launch.

What BidPilot is built for

BidPilot is designed to help businesses prepare quotations faster, track internal pricing more clearly, organise customer and job details, and keep accepted work moving with cleaner structure.

It is being shaped for trades and contracting workflows where speed matters, but so does accuracy.
